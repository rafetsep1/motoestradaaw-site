MotoEstradaAW Publisher

Schedule and publish your own short videos to your own TikTok account and YouTube channel.

What it does

MotoEstradaAW Publisher is a desktop tool used by motorcycle-travel creators to plan their short-video calendar. After the creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, they can, for each video:

  • see the TikTok account the video will be posted to (nickname and avatar);
  • edit the title, hashtags and caption;
  • choose who can see the video (from the options TikTok allows for that account — nothing is pre-selected);
  • choose whether to allow comments, Duet and Stitch (all off by default);
  • disclose promotional or branded content;
  • publish now, or schedule a date and time, and follow the upload status.

Videos are only sent to TikTok after the creator explicitly chooses to publish or schedule them. The tool never posts to an account that has not authorized it and never adds watermarks or branding to the creator's content.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic — to show which account is connected.
  • video.publish — to publish the videos the creator selected, with the settings the creator chose.

YouTube

The same tool uploads the creator's own videos to the creator's own YouTube channel, using YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3). After the creator connects the channel with Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0), they can, for each video:

  • see the YouTube channel the video will be uploaded to (channel name and handle);
  • edit the title, description and tags;
  • choose the privacy (public, unlisted or private) and disclose altered or synthetic content;
  • upload now, or schedule it together with the other platforms, and follow the upload status and the link to the video.

Scopes used: youtube.upload (upload the videos the creator selected) and youtube.readonly (show which channel is connected). The creator can disconnect at any time in the tool, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in Google security settings.
